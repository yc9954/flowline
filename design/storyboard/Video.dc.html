Production notes
1 · Voice first. Record the VO, lay it on the timeline, cut screens to it.
2 · One run. Cuts 04–10 come from one simulation run so every number agrees.
3 · One live take. Cut 07 is the agent on a real Daytona desktop. Record a backup take.
4 · Honest labels. “Simulated team” once in cut 02. The only real-data number is 7.4× vs 5.7× (US Census backtest).
5 · Motion rules. Ease-out 300–500 ms. Never move camera and UI at the same time. Supers in for ≥ 2 s.
6 · Bookend. End on cut 01’s frame so the thesis is the last thing judges see.
