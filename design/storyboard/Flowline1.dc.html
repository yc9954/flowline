Flowline-1 · design Northwind Fitness · v15

A model of how one company works — built from what its people approve, tested before it acts.

Not a fine-tuned LLM. Flowline-1 is the company’s own knowledge, simulator and demand model; agents use it as their tool, and it is recalibrated every night.

What is new6
01Process data without system logs
Process mining needs event logs from ERP systems. Small, non-engineering teams have none. Flowline-1 builds the log from the desktop — and every line was approved by the person who did the work.
02An ontology nobody configures
It assembles itself every night from records, merging the same thing called by different names, and keeps every version.
03Counterfactuals on your own company
Dashboards show what happened. Flowline-1 replays the same team, requests and luck under each plan, so a change is tested before anyone changes.
04Decisions routed, not displayed
One knowledge, many surfaces. The CEO gets a line, finance an approval, a trainer a two-minute review. Nobody has to open a dashboard.
05Calibrated, and says what it can’t call
Every prediction is checked against what happened. Out of range, it stops suggesting. Weak spots are on screen.
06Learns from yes, no and undo
Each answer tunes who gets asked what; each undo fixes the skill. The model improves with use, not with retraining.
